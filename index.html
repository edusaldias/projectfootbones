Sex Estimation Calculator

By: Esteban Arroyo and Eduardo Saldías

Methodology based on Chilean Subactual Osteological Series. Paper: "Sexual dimorphism in the cuneiform bones from a Chilean subactual osteological series" (Alvarez, I. Arroyo, E. Retamal, R. Saldías, E.).

Requirements: The Excel file must contain the specific headings needed for the selected model (e.g., MW1, MW2, LFM2, WFM1, LFN2, ML3, MW3) without missing or 'NA' rows.
