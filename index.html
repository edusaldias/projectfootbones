Sex Estimation Calculator

Methodology based on Chilean Subactual Osteological Series (Akaike Model Selection). Proposal from the paper titled "Sexual dimorphism in the cuneiform bones from a Chilean subactual osteological series" (Alvarez, Isabela; Arroyo, Esteban; Retamal, Rodrigo; Saldías, Eduardo). All measurements must be in millimeters.
